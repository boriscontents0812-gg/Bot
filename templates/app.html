1. Projects — Create or open a project before starting. Projects save your script, audio clips, contact photos, images and all settings. Max 5 projects per account.

2. Script format — First line is the contact name — it appears in the iMessage header at the top of the screen. Then each message: 1: VoiceName > message (received, left bubble) or 2: VoiceName > message (sent, right bubble).

3. Contact photo — Upload a profile picture for each contact in the left panel. The photo appears as the avatar in the header and next to received bubbles. The contact name must match exactly.

4. Image bubbles — Use 1: img: photo1 or 2: img: photo1 to insert an image bubble. Upload the image via the script image uploader. The name must match exactly (case-sensitive).

5. Display vs voice text — Use == to send different text to ElevenLabs than what appears on screen: 1: Natasha > Hello == Hello!! — "Hello" shows in the bubble, "Hello!!" is spoken.

6. Multiple contacts — Add a new contact name line anywhere in the script to start a new chat screen. Each non-message line acts as a group separator and sets the contact name for the next block.

7. Bubble settings — In the right panel under Bubble design adjust scale, width, corner radius, tail size, colors and sent/received bubble fade. Changes update the preview in real time.

8. Header settings — Under Header in the right panel, toggle persistent header, adjust avatar size, and set the battery/signal display. The header contact name comes from the script.

9. Sponsor overlays — Three-line blocks for animated overlays. Use rizz, plug, or wing as the first line, then the overlay voice name, then the message line. rizz shows the Rizz card overlay, plug shows the Plug card, wing shows the Wing card — each plays the intro phrase spoken by the given voice, then the message appears in chat with its normal voice.

10. ElevenLabs & audio — Add your API key in the left panel, pick a voice profile, then hit Generate Audio. Voice names must match your ElevenLabs account (case-insensitive). Review clips in the Audio tab and regenerate individual lines if needed.

11. Music & gameplay — Upload background music (mp3/m4a/wav/ogg) and gameplay video (mp4) in the Video settings panel (max 500 MB). Set start time with 0:00 format. Toggle music fade-out and end fade to black.

12. Generate video — Hit Generate Video in the right panel. If it exceeds 2:59, use Shorten to 2:59 to auto-compress to exactly 2:59. Download from the Video tab.
